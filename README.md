## how to play

for the basic game click play then click and hold while moving your mouse to carve out shapes trying to match the shape of the face in the display above. Then click submit to see your score and how to improve, as the green shows success, gray shows misses, and red show areas where you drew out of the lines. Then try again or click next for a new face. Keep in mind some faces will be harder than others. Once your ready for a challenge try medium, hard or insane (i can get 100% on hard consistently), once you got the hang of the base game you should try timed which doesn't allow retries and only gives you 10 seconds. 

(normal acts as the casual game mode while timed is the competitive mode. )

> tip: 
> the color of the face matters and the color in the display is the color your fire needs to be.
> matters a lot.
